Course Overview

This intensive course is designed for aspiring network engineers eager to enhance their understanding of Cisco networking concepts and services. Focusing on core areas such as routers and switches, the course integrates theoretical learning with practical lab exercises using Packet Tracer.

Course Highlights

The curriculum covers a wide range of topics, including IPv4 and IPv6 subnetting, IP routing (static and dynamic), and inter-VLAN routing. You will delve into advanced subjects like EIGRP, OSPF, and IPsec VPN while exploring NAT and ACLs for network security. Additionally, learn the intricacies of WAN protocols and troubleshooting techniques to solidify your skills.

What You'll Learn

Understand core Cisco networking concepts.
Perform subnetting and routing configurations.
Configure and understand EIGRP, OSPF, and NAT.
Use IPv6 efficiently in modern networks.
Implement inter-VLAN routing and troubleshoot networks.
Set up VPNs and manage network security with ACLs.
